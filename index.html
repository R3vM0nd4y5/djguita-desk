How this works

Three words you will see

MUSEDASH is your dashboard. THE DREAMLINE is the engine room that makes it and these pages. Dova's DREAMGLASS is the tab inside MUSEDASH where your list, your files, your map and your tickets live.

Your buttons and your tickets

Next to each item of your list sit three buttons: done, question, blocked. Press one and your mail app opens with the subject already written; type a line and send. It lands with Jeffrey and shows on your dashboard. “Send a new ticket” works the same way; mark it LOW, MED, HIGH or CRITICAL so he knows what to read first.

Live pages and snapshots

A live page carries a green banner with the time it was made and rebuilds itself. A snapshot carries a gold banner with the day it was written and does not change; for today, open the dashboard.

Your map

Your list as a road, coloured by where each item stands, lives in MUSEDASH under “Your map” and inside Dova's DREAMGLASS under “The map”.

These pages refresh on their own. MUSEDASH names the date of the numbers it carries. Nothing is released, posted or spent without your yes.
