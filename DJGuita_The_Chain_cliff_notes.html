3The highlights

  • viryl + intellijend are one company, two tools: make, then place. intellijend runs ads from your own Meta account, so the data stays yours. Use them, learn one full cycle, then we run the same shape from your account with no subscription.
  • Your Meta connection is the one blocker. Their guide read 0 of 5 steps on Nov 7: video; beneficiary and payer; share the Instagram profile with the ad account; intellijend in Events Manager's allowed domains; automatic adjustments off. Their $30 setup call is there if you want it.
  • Aura and Boost Collective do not fit. Aura is a reseller (20% fee; about 110 non-programmed streams on Angel Face). Boost sells placement against Spotify's rules. Let anything paid run out while we measure it; do not renew.
  • Demons In My Closet: Alex's played master is what makes the song sellable. A fully AI-made recording cannot be registered as yours; a human-performed one can. The 35% was a capped test deal on one song; your terms stay (no open-ended points, about 20% the ceiling). You want a cuatro part; that belongs in this step.
  • Your dashboard is live. MUSEDASH re-renders every half hour and shows the date of its numbers. Next, your own Spotify exports flow in automatically (nothing touches your account, nothing is scraped). Keep the Made-with-AI label: Spotify carried Brujeria with it.
